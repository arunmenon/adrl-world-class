## 3. Would the deck and reports lead a leader to overinterpret?

The written qualifications are unusually thorough. The overinterpretation risk comes from titles, tables and numbers that travel without their footnotes. Specific phrases:

- **"18 / 18 recorded tool actions matched"** (slide 4, large figure). Reads as a reliability metric. It is one session of one task in hook-only mode.
- **Chart "Correct examples: Earlier checks 4, Proposed checks 7"** (slide 5). A bar chart of seven author-written variants invites "75 percent improvement" arithmetic. The footer qualifies it; the chart does not.
- **"Recovery now passes the controlled job tests"** and **"13 test jobs accounted for and removed"** (slide 6). Reads as a resilience result for the product. It is a static binary in a container.
- **"Today's learning is a disciplined engineering process"** (slide 11). The word learning attaches to an ML product. Nothing has learned anything.
- **"All 77 decision grades remain unchanged"** (slide 8). Reads as "the architecture is validated". It means the ledger was not edited; it includes stale shadow grades.
- **"No immediate input blocks the next offline work"** (slide 12, PDF, executive review). Reads as "no decisions needed". Four leadership decisions block the value proof.
- **"Building dependable oversight for AI coding work"** (slide 1). The product's oversight today is observation of tool events plus synthetic gate fixtures.
- **"Then we repeat the workflow with another coding tool and measure reliability, quality, effort and cost"** (slide 12). Reads as next quarter's plan; none of its inputs exist.
- **The 895 figure and "all 11 engineering checks"** repeated in five documents. Test counts are the most quoted number in the record, and the record itself says they "do not measure percent product completion".
- **"$4,000 gross ... $1,500 net"** and **"$270,000"** in the brief. Labelled invented, but they are the only dollar figures a reader will remember.
- **"served: claude-fable-5-1, gateway_reported"** in the linked evidence table. Looks like a provider receipt from a real model.
- **"Wave 3 in progress"** while the journey's current position is W7.0a. A leader tracking waves will place the program one wave behind or ahead depending on which document they open.

The routing report says the earlier deck "blurred that distinction" between infrastructure and routing progress. The deck was not withdrawn or annotated after that finding; the executive review received a one-paragraph header instead.
